Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Merge Quest mobile game ("the app") handles and why. Merge Quest is a single-player puzzle game. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal details to play.

1. Information stored on your device

Your board, level, currencies, discoveries, daily missions, expedition progress, play time and settings (sound, vibration, visual theme) are saved only on your device. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account so it can be restored on a new device. This is controlled by Google and your device settings, and we cannot access it.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy > Ad Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Rewarded video ads are always optional.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Save backups you create

The "Backup & Save Recovery" feature copies your save data to your device's clipboard as text. It is only created when you tap the button, and it is not sent anywhere unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

4. What we do not collect

The app does not use analytics, does not require sign-in, and does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone. It uses the internet only to load ads.

5. Children

Merge Quest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your choices and data requests

You can change your ad consent choice in Settings, reset your advertising ID in your device settings, reset your progress in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data. For any privacy question or request, email us.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
