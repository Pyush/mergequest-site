Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Merge Quest mobile game ("the app") handles and why. Merge Quest is a single-player puzzle game. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal details to play.

1. Information stored on your device

Your board, level, currencies, discoveries, daily missions, daily login streak, expedition progress, play time, settings (sound, vibration, visual theme, reminders) and a record of which purchases have been delivered are saved only on your device. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account so it can be restored on a new device. This is controlled by Google and your device settings, and we cannot access it.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy > Ad Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Rewarded video ads are always optional. If you buy Remove Ads, banner and interstitial ads stop; rewarded videos remain available if you choose to watch them.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn how the game is played, improve it and decide where ads fit best, the app sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • levels reached, patron orders delivered and chapters completed
  • items discovered for the first time (item, family and tier)
  • challenge rifts started and rewards claimed, and ascensions
  • limited-time offers shown, ads shown, and the outcome of rewarded ads you chose to watch
  • in-app purchases (product and price, reported by Google Play; never your payment details)
  • taps on Share, on the "More apps" screen and on in-app update prompts
  • whether Remove Ads is active

Google also collects an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version, approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address) and, where available, the advertising ID. These statistics are not used to identify you, and Google keeps them for a limited period under the Google Analytics data retention settings. Where the law requires consent for ads (for example in the EEA and UK), Google Analytics follows the choice you make in the consent form, which you can change under Settings > Privacy > Ad Privacy Choices.

Learn how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. "More apps" list and sharing

The "More apps" screen shows our other apps. Once a day the app downloads the list from ultimateplaystudio.com/apps.json (formerly pyush.github.io/apps.json), a public file on GitHub Pages; like any web request, this reveals your IP address to GitHub, and no other information is sent. To show "Open" instead of "Get", the app checks on your device whether any of our own apps are installed; it can only see those apps, and the result never leaves your device. Tapping an app opens it or its Google Play page.

"Share Merge Quest" opens your device's share sheet with a short message and a Google Play link. You choose whether and where to send it; the app does not see who you share with.

5. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as gems, the Starter Pack and Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play shares the purchase with the app (for example the product, order identifier and purchase status) so the app can deliver what you bought, and the app keeps a record of delivered purchases on your device to avoid delivering them twice. Purchases are tied to your Google account under Google Payments' privacy notice.

6. Reminders

If you turn them on, the app schedules local notifications on your device to tell you when your energy is full or your daily reward is ready. They are created and delivered by your device; no information is sent to us or any server. You can turn them off in Settings or in your device's notification settings.

7. Save backups you create

The "Backup & Save Recovery" feature copies your save data to your device's clipboard as text. It is only created when you tap the button, and it is not sent anywhere unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

8. What we do not collect

The app does not require sign-in and does not access your contacts, photos, precise location, camera or microphone. It uses the internet only to load ads, to process purchases through Google Play, to send the usage statistics described in section 3, to download the "More apps" list (section 4) and to check Google Play for updates.

9. Children

Merge Quest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Your choices and data requests

You can change your ad consent choice in Settings, reset your advertising ID in your device settings, turn reminders on or off in Settings, reset your progress in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data. For any privacy question or request, email us.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
